# SFX Pack (2026-10-05): ability sounds + haunted music

A zip Holden supplied on 2026-10-05 (`animegirlsoverrealones.zip`, plus three loose `Zenith_*_v4.ogg` files), described as being about creating SFX for Roblox games. **Holden: "those are references"** (2026-10-05), so this is study material, not his own audio.

## TL;DR
- **Safe.** 44 audio files only (36 Ogg Vorbis, 8 MP3), checked by header bytes and file type. No executables, scripts or hidden files, and no paths outside the folder. The zip passes its integrity test and has no comment. The three loose Zenith files are byte-identical to the zipped copies.
- **What it is:**
  - **7 anime-style ability sound sets** (Zenith, Ascend, Awaken, Galaxy, Starfall, Storm, Tornado), several in multiple versions
  - a **prize-wheel** set
  - 3 one-shots (fuse, ghost drop-off, secret door)
  - **6 haunted-theme music tracks** (77–116 s)
  - There's **no written guide** in the zip: the "how to make SFX" lessons below are read out of the sounds themselves.
- **The anatomy every ability set follows:**
  1. **windup**: a riser that gets louder and brighter and **ends at its peak**, so the hit takes over
  2. **hit**: fast attack (mostly 40–240 ms), then a 2–3.4 s darkening tail
  3. **aura**: a 4 s bed with matching start and end levels, built to **loop**
- **Zenith v1 → v4 shows the iteration:** v4 moved energy out of the sub-bass into the mids (beam: sub share 88% → 54%), hit faster (beam attack 750 → 140 ms) and sits quieter (beam 9.5 dB below v3, windup 7 dB below _1). That's the right direction for **phone speakers**, which play little real bass (⚠️ verify on a device).
- **Fix before use** (check by ear):
  - Awaken_Build and Tornado_Form **start late** (69 / 107 ms).
  - AwakenBlast and StormCharge **clip** slightly.
  - Zenith_Beam_v4 **ends while still audible** (possible click).
  - Several loops have **seam jumps** (Zenith/Awaken auras 4–6%, music `hall_depths` 12%, `graveyard_shuffle` 7%).
- **These are references (Holden, 2026-10-05):** study them; don't ship or upload them unless the creator and licence are confirmed. If it was made with an AI generator, Holden's rule for music is no AI tracks. Only upload the **final** versions: 44 files would use 44 of the 100/month audio quota.

## Breakdown: how these ability sounds are built
1. **Three phases, made as separate files** and chained in game:
   - **Windup / charge / form** (1.2–6.7 s): gets louder over its length (end 8–51 dB louder than its start) and, in 8 of the 10 windup/charge/form/open sounds, **brighter** (rising sweep). It **ends at its loudest**: windups stop at −6 to −14 dB on purpose, because the hit is meant to start right there.
   - **Hit / beam / strike / blast / impact** (0.9–3.6 s): **fast attack (mostly 40–240 ms; StormBlast 420, older Zenith beams 300–750)**, then a long tail that **darkens as it decays** (falling sweep in most hits) and fades 32–59 dB down.
   - **Aura / roar / storm bed** (3–4 s): **level-flat** (start and end within about 2 dB), designed to **loop** under a held state.
2. **Extra beats for combos:** Awaken's second set adds a **lunge** (0.62 s, 39 ms attack, energy in 2.5–6 kHz, the "swish" range) and a **finisher** (2.6 s, bright), and Tornado has form → roar → touch → fade. A full ability is 3–5 files.
3. **Bass is where the weight is**, and the risk. Big hits put 90–97% of their energy below 150 Hz (StormBlast, AwakenBlast, Starfall_Hit). On headphones that's huge, but ⚠️ phone speakers reproduce very little that low, so these could feel thin on mobile. Verify on a real phone.
4. **The v4 revision pass** shows what the creator fixed (Zenith):

   **What v4 changed:**
   - **less sub, more mids.** v3 had gone fully into bass (88% sub).
   - **a much faster hit** (140 ms).
   - **a soft first sample.** v1–v3 begin with a 33–78% jump, which can click.
   - **a windup that peaks closer to the very end.**
   - **quieter** (beam 9.5 dB below v3, windup 7.3 dB below _1), leaving headroom.

   The new problem in v4: the beam **no longer fades out** (ends at −29 dB, where earlier versions reached about −56 dB).

## Checklist: making ability SFX for Roblox (derived from this pack + vault rules)
- [ ] Split each ability into windup → hit → aura (+ lunge / finisher) files and chain them in code ([[Roblox Sound Library Skill]] SoundPlan layers with delays).
- [ ] **Windup:** rising loudness and brightness, ends at its peak exactly where the hit starts.
- [ ] **Hit:** attack within about 40–250 ms (most of this pack's hits), then a tail that **fades to silence** (no cut end; add a 50 ms fade-out).
- [ ] **Loop beds:** equal start and end level and a near-zero seam jump (Ascend_Aura's 0.1% is the model). Crossfade the loop point otherwise.
- [ ] **No silence at the start** (< 50 ms) and **no clipping** (peak ≤ −1 dBFS).
- [ ] **Keep real energy in the mids (600 Hz–6 kHz)** so it reads on phone speakers. Use sub-bass as the extra weight, not the whole sound.
- [ ] **Measure before approving:** `soundcheck.py` (limits, late start, clipping, suggested volume) and `sfxprofile.py` (shape, sheets). Then Holden listens.
- [ ] **Upload only the final versions** (quota: 100/month if ID-verified, 10 if not).

## Pitfalls
- **Unknown creator and licence**, and possibly AI-made (the audio can't tell us). Don't upload or ship until Holden confirms the source. His rule is no AI-generated music.
- This pack is studied, not shipped. The haunted music and ability sets suit specific game themes; don't add them to a game by default.
- Seam and click findings are measurements. Confirm by ear before re-editing.
