# X Reference: VFX

52 Roblox VFX posts from X (#RobloxVFX, sorted by likes, 2024-06 onward plus a few seeds). Part of [[X-Reference-Library]].
Most #RobloxVFX volume is anime-combat effects. Those are kept for technique, but the most relevant posts for Holden's
games are the cosmetic, environmental, pickup, pet and indicator effects.

## TL;DR
- **Cute cosmetics beat combat in reach:** DKEaterrr's Fisch 'Lovely Boats' (crescent-moon boats, soft glow, sparkles,
  rainbow nebula wake) had 11k likes, more than almost any combat effect. Cosmetic VFX should look great standing still,
  because that's when players show them off.
- **Pickups read through colour + icon + glow + bob** (BardVfx_x resource orbs). Use one consistent colour per resource.
- **Five-beat reward moment** (BardVfx_x pet bond): sparkle → ring flash → icon grows with aura → hold → particle burst,
  about 1.5 s. Reuse it for level-ups, pet affection and rebirth.
- **Ground indicators** (RGUYW, justhazu TD indicators): flat glowing decals (circles, cones, rectangles) with themed
  edges; they tell the player where to stand or place.
- **Environmental VFX** (ChipTomatoberry pack, starfall_wav, Squall weathers) sell biome identity cheaply: falling leaves,
  lava surfaces, cloud vortexes. Matches 'crisp zone colours' in [[Art-Direction]].
- **Auras are a monetisable cosmetic** in RNG games (Sols RNG submission, BeccauRblx 'aura in 30 minutes').

## Pitfalls
- Many showcases are filmed at max graphics on PC. Budget particle counts for phones ([[VFX-Particles-Beams-Trails]]).
- Combat VFX here leans on anime IP (Forsaken, JJK, One Piece). Fine for technique study, but never ship IP references.
