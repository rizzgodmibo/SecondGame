# X Reference: Low-Poly Builds, Lobbies and Maps

23 posts: low-poly models, builds and maps, plus lobby and map showcases (2026-10-04). Part of [[X-Reference-Library]].
Holden's art rules (flat-shaded low-poly Blender assets, crisp zone colours, colour variation, varied trees; no AI meshes)
are in `C:\Users\holde\Documents\GameDev\AssetLibrary\README.md` and [[Art-Direction]].

## TL;DR
- **Closest match to Paper Plane Toss's hub:** Orca_Environ's pastel lagoon (teal lollipop trees, sandy cliffs, pastel
  sea, islands at varied heights). The repeated tree shape changes in size and tilt, and the water is one soft hue.
- **Function readable from the silhouette:** ayzenrbx's blacksmith hut shows a hammer-and-saw sign on the roof and a glowing
  forge, so you know what it is without UI.
- **Lead the eye with a path to a glowing goal** (haooffiso's fantasy city hub; OmniKoi2's jungle canyon frames its path with cliffs). Frame paths with cliffs or
  tree walls.
- **256px textures are enough** for low-poly characters (1,800 polys + 256px, snj_esk). Supports small texture budgets in
  [[Blender-To-Roblox-Pipeline]].
- **Colour variants of one mesh** (M0TOPRINCESS corbels in 4 colours) is the cheap way to get model colour variation.
- **Seasonal props in a matching style:** hand-painted low-poly jack-o'-lanterns in three sizes with different faces.
- **asimo3089's uncopylocked places** (in [[X-Game-Feel-And-Showcases]] → Tools) include a full lobby; open them in Studio
  to study lobby layout and lighting.

## Pitfalls
- Several 'low-poly' posts use hand-painted textures (pumpkins, chest); that's a different look from Holden's flat-shaded
  vertex-colour style. Use them for shape and composition only.
- Lobby and map showcases are filmed with cinematic lighting; check readability under the game's real lighting on a phone.
