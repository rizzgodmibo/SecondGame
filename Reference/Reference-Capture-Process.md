# Reference Capture Process

## TL;DR
- Start in [[X-Reference-Library]] and its manifests; deduplicate by post ID.
- Preserve media, creator credit, permalink, post date, capture date and inspection method.
- Likes and polished mockups do not establish working UX or commercial success.

## Procedure (maintenance recommendations)
1. Prefer the creator's original post. Record credited collaborators without extending that attribution to unrelated work.
2. Select a concrete lesson: layout, state transition, icon family, reveal or feedback. Include device and genre context; seek contrasting approaches as well as popular posts.
3. Preserve available files with their real format and resolution. Distinguish screenshots, posters, contact sheets and video. If export fails, keep the permalink and blocker; check the archive before duplicating downloads.
4. Record post ID, author, dates, source URL, local files, resolution limitations, rights status and SHA-256 in a manifest.
5. Inspect saved media. Label evidence as still inspected, sampled frames inspected, playback reviewed or caption only. Contact sheets can miss animation timing. Record timestamps only when measured.
6. Write Observation, Reusable principle and Critique/limit. Separate creator claims from evidence and implementation proposals from approved decisions.
7. Audit mockup numbers: sum mutually exclusive probabilities, compute unit prices, distinguish currencies and identify placeholder labels or deadlines. Never copy a reference economy without checking it.
8. Update category notes and the hub. Generated catalogue corrections must also update `takeaways.json`; handwritten supplements belong outside generated blocks.

## Checklist
- [ ] Source identity retained and duplicate check complete.
- [ ] Saved files open and match the reference; existence is not playback verification.
- [ ] Resolution, inspection method and missing evidence are explicit.
- [ ] Third-party study media stays local; verify ignore rules include its file format before any separate publishing task.
- [ ] Phone readability, input, animation interruption and reduced-motion behavior remain pending until tested in the implementation.
- [ ] Sources, wikilinks and maintenance log updated.

## Pitfalls
- A showcase does not verify purchase receipts, failure recovery, performance or retention.
- A screenshot cannot establish easing, audio or hover behavior.
- A caption's conversion claim is not measured conversion data.
- Study access does not supply a production asset licence.
