# X Reference: Thumbnails, Icons and the AI-Thumbnail Debate

25 posts from X search `roblox thumbnail min_faves:300` (2026-10-04): community sentiment on AI thumbnails, human-made
thumbnails for big games, event badges, and two widely shared first-impression threads. Part of [[X-Reference-Library]].
Platform facts about thumbnails (sizes, personalization, policy) live in [[Thumbnails-And-Icons]]; this note is community
evidence only.

## TL;DR
- **The anti-AI-thumbnail backlash is the loudest thumbnail topic on Roblox X.** The top post ('if it has an AI generated
  thumbnail I'm not playing it') has 46.5k likes, the most-liked post in the whole library. Replies call out AI thumbnails
  by name (2.7k, 549 likes), list them with 'botted player counts' as scam signals (6k), and artists show AI remakes of
  their work (584). One studio now publicly promises human-only artists under contract.
- **What that does and doesn't prove:** it's sentiment among X users (devs and older players), not CTR data from young-teen
  players. It is still a reputational risk: AI art gets noticed and mocked. Treat 'AI vs in-engine render vs human art' as
  something to A/B test with [[Thumbnails-And-Icons]] personalization, not a settled answer.
- **Nostalgia for genuine avatar renders:** 17.7k likes for 'make thumbnails like these again' on a classic in-engine
  render (real Roblox avatars posed in a lit scene with a bold logo).
- **Human-made thumbnails for top games share a recipe:** 1–3 characters huge in the foreground, a strong diagonal action
  line, saturated colour with an effects layer (fire, water, music notes), the logo in a corner, and the game's promise in
  one glance (a barrel + waterfall; lots of cute pets).
- **Event badges:** during The Hunt: Roblox 20, NDS and Piggy added a red '20' badge or card frame to their thumbnail and
  icon. A cheap way to signal 'in the event / updated' in the discovery row.
- **First impression is everything ('Timmy'):** players don't read descriptions or sit through tutorials; if the loop
  isn't obvious from the thumbnail and the first 5 seconds, they leave. jandel (Grow a Garden) says icon + thumbnail drive
  CTR and D1 for the algorithm, and warns against giving equity to marketers or YouTubers (self-reported advice).

## Decision rule (recommendation)
If a thumbnail candidate is AI-generated, ship it only as one arm of a personalization test against a non-AI version
(in-engine render or human art) and judge on qualified play-through, per [[Thumbnails-And-Icons]]. For Paper Plane Toss
this is flagged in [[Paper Plane Toss Thumbnails and Game Icon]]; the decision is Holden's.
