# X Reference: Game Feel, Showcases, Events, Tech and Art

37 of the highest-liked #RobloxDev posts (2,000+ likes, curated) plus a few seeds: game feel, viral game reveals,
event trailers, tech demos, art and tools. Part of [[X-Reference-Library]].

## TL;DR
- **FadedB_Fox's 'Scrapbook Saga' is the standout reference** (24k / 19k / 8.5k likes): a paper-craft Paper Mario-style
  RPG. Its UI is diegetic paper: diagonal paper-strip 'New Item!' toasts, a folder + paper-clip inventory with polaroid
  previews, paper-tag HUD pills, sewing-button currency bursting from crates, and collage-panel battle transitions.
  The theme is directly relevant to [[Paper Plane Toss]] (recommendation, not an approved direction).
- **Pickup flow worth copying:** announce the item (banner with name + one-line effect) → show where it went (it flies into
  the bag icon) → let the player open the bag to see it.
- **Colour-ramp rule (RhosGFX, 4.2k likes):** darker shades move the hue toward blue, raise saturation and lower
  brightness; lighter shades do the reverse. Example cube: H129 S37 B94 / H165 S91 B78 / H179 S99 B62.
- **Reveals that go viral are one strong image:** the LAST STOP logo (stop sign with a monster face), a Mario Party-style
  board on Roblox, the Egg Hunt 2026 cinematic trailer.
- **The Hatch post-mortem (events):** Roblox's July 2025 egg hunt became the worst-rated platform event. 60% of the 1,000
  games dropped out, it had a 21% like ratio, inappropriate games slipped in, and the community counter was reportedly a
  disguised timer. A community Egg Hunt 2026 was also downvoted, for paywalling. Summary added to [[Events-And-Seasons]].
- **asimo3089 uncopylocked most of his solo work** (Tools section): professional places, including a lobby, to open and learn from.
- **Growth claims in posts are self-reported** (e.g. '4M plays in a day'). Log them as anecdotes only
  ([[Growth-Metrics-And-Benchmarks]]).

### Art lessons (1)

#### RhosGFX — how to choose colours
[post](https://x.com/RhosGFX/status/2028832771754664277) · ♥ 4,250 · 2026-03-03
![[Assets/Reference-Captures/X/art-lessons/RhosGFX-2028832771754664277/img-1.jpg|480]]
> Recently had someone ask how i choose my colours. it's pretty simple: start with…
**Take:** Viewed. The colour-ramp rule with real values from the example cube: light top H129 S37 B94 → base front H165 S91 B78 → shadow side H179 S99 B62. Each step darker moves the hue about 15–35° toward blue/cyan, raises saturation and drops brightness about 16. Going lighter does the reverse (hue toward yellow, less saturation, more brightness). Use it for vertex-colour or material shades on low-poly models instead of just darkening one colour; it gives the crisp, varied zone colours in [[Art-Direction]].
