# X Reference: HUDs and Menus

42 HUD and menu posts from X (daily rewards, battlepass, quests, results screens, map vote, inventory, codes). Part of
[[X-Reference-Library]]. Media is local in `Assets/Reference-Captures/X/`.

## TL;DR
- **The '+1 / simulator' HUD template** (mlaskaczz, Minitrot1, Livvyjuju): a 2x3 square icon grid on the left (Shop,
  Trails/Auras, Pets, Rebirth, Invite); a Spin wheel badge top-right; a right column with a limited-offer timer card, a 2x
  boost and Auto-win; the main stat counter + level/XP bar + multiplier at bottom centre; a 'Friend Boost +X%' chip. This
  is the closest genre template for [[Paper Plane Toss]].
- **Lobby docks** (cricketrblx, Zac1kio, oreuiz): one wide primary button (PLAY / BATTLE) flanked by colour-coded square
  icons with labels underneath. Social proof such as 'GLOBAL PLAYERS: 12,583' goes right under PLAY.
- **Daily rewards** (AltroUIUX): a 30-day horizontal track, a special card every few days, a streak chip with a flame,
  CLAIM → CLAIMED. Pairs with [[Daily-Rewards-And-Streaks]].
- **Results screens are offer slots:** Round Complete → 'Repair Base ($5000)'; Victory → +coins strip with a brighter
  Play Again than Lobby.
- **Server-event alerts:** a timer + a striped banner ('Lightning Storm — Seek Shelter Immediately!') turns a mechanic
  into a moment.
- **Expressive status:** the circular HP ring around an avatar whose face changes with HP (11.9k likes) beats a plain bar
  for readability on mobile.
