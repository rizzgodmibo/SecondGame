# X Reference: Egg Hatching and Pet Systems

15 posts on egg hatching from X (2026-10-04): Adopt Me's redesigned hatch, indie hatch animations, rarity-escalating
reveals, BIG Games' hatch battles, and pet-system kits. Part of [[X-Reference-Library]]. For the event side of eggs
(The Hatch post-mortem) see [[X-Game-Feel-And-Showcases]]; for in-shop reveal recipes see [[X-Shop-And-Seasonal-UI]].

## TL;DR
- **Two hatch styles:** a *world hatch* (Adopt Me: the egg cracks where it sits, next to the player, pure VFX with no UI
  frame) versus a *screen hatch* (simulators: dim the screen, egg to centre, shake, reveal card). The world hatch feels
  calmer and more 'pet-like'; the screen hatch gets more hype per pull.
- **Escalate by rarity.** Adopt Me's legendary hatch is longer, adds sparkles and changes the ring colour (orange basic →
  purple legendary). Players read the tier before they see the pet. Use the same beats with more intensity, not a
  different animation per tier.
- **The Adopt Me beat sheet** (by VisuallyFX): light pillar with the egg in silhouette → radial burst ring + shell shards
  → starburst → pet appears → white diagonal wipe to the next hatch.
- **Hatching as a social or competitive mechanic:** BIG Games' 'Hatch Battle' (hatch to beat bosses, Team Orange vs
  Blue, risk-your-streak luck). ⚠️ Streak-risk luck is gambling-adjacent; avoid it for young teens.
- **Pet-system kits** exist free (Discord-tag gated) and paid (about 10k R$). Treat them as unvetted third-party code; Holden
  already has reviewed rarity and size roll code in Fish a Monster.

## Checklist for a hatch (derived; untested in Holden's games)
- [ ] Pick a world or screen hatch to fit the game's tone.
- [ ] 4–5 beats, under about 2 s for common pets; add one extra beat and colour for each rarity step.
- [ ] Show the odds before purchase and the name + rarity on reveal ([[X-Shop-And-Seasonal-UI]]).
- [ ] Batch hatches reveal one by one with an 'N of M' counter and a skip button.
- [ ] Reuse the five-beat reward moment from [[X-VFX-Reference]] for pet level-ups.

## Pitfalls
- Paid random pets need the odds disclosed (⚠️ verify current Roblox rules; open item in [[Gap-Tracker]]).
- Long unskippable hatch animations frustrate repeat buyers; always allow a skip after the first view.
