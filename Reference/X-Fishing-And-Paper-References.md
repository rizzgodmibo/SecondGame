# X Reference: Fishing Games and Paper Style

Genre references for Holden's two projects: 10 fishing-game posts (for [[Fish a Monster]]) and 2 paper-style posts (for
[[Paper Plane Toss]]), captured 2026-10-04. Part of [[X-Reference-Library]]. The strongest paper-style reference,
FadedB_Fox's *Scrapbook Saga*, is in [[X-Game-Feel-And-Showcases]].

## TL;DR — fishing
- **A fishing game can top Roblox:** Fisch hit #1 by CCU (825.82k shown) about two months after its October 2024 launch.
- **The loop everyone copies is cast → sell → bigger rod → repeat.** Developers now pitch their twist against it ('Schedule 1
  + Fisch, plus wacky devices that raise fish value'). Fish a Monster should be able to state its twist in one line
  (recommendation; the design is Holden's call).
- **Variation is the collection hook:** Fisch's pitch is '400,000+ possible variations' per fish (mutations, sizes),
  matching Fish a Monster's rarity + size rolls ([[Fish a Monster Catch and Reel Design]]).
- **Seasonal loop for fishing** (Fish It Christmas): presents from door-to-door or rare catches, exchanged for rewards,
  a seasonal battlepass, and a cave that opens for 30 minutes every 2 hours.
- **Gifting catches makes social moments** (a stranger gifting an orca, 508 likes).
- **Code to study:** littensy's open-source fishing minigame (Charm state management). Check the licence.
- **Watch:** Roblox's official interview with Fisch's developer on building a 'cozy, competitive world'.

## TL;DR — paper style
- A Paper Mario-style 2D-sprite set made for a Roblox game got 7.5k likes; paper-in-3D is a recognised, loved look.
- Searches for paper/notebook/scrapbook UI found little beyond Scrapbook Saga, so the niche is open on Roblox
  (observation from one search, not market research).
