# Art Direction Feedback

Holden's standing preferences, taken from his critiques in [[Fish a Monster Map Iterations]] and [[Paper Plane Toss UI Redesign]].

## Rejected
- Roblox's AI mesh generator, and props built from parts. "Low quality, outdated."
- Realistic terrain style for these games.
- **Flat single-colour models and floors.** "One dimensional, stale."
- Repetitive trees.
- **Blurry gradient or noise ground.** "Like a smudged mess."
- **UI built from primitives.** "Lazy." Too dark, boxed icons, thin outlines.
- Lighting that's too bright and washed out (the sky island hub).

## Wanted
- Clean, flat-shaded low-poly with **crisp zone colours and clean curved borders**. "Smooth" meant *clean*, not blurred.
- Colour variation on every model, varied tree species, size and tint.
- Chunky rounded boulders, layered tree canopies, faceted rock cliffs.
- Bright, saturated, "clickable" UI made from painted image assets, copying the reference games closely.
- Honest critique of weak points with every render, and renders shown **before** uploading.

## Logo and discovery artwork feedback (2026-10-03)

- Holden explicitly loved the Codex gold/cyan sculpted Paper Plane Toss title, with an integrated folded plane and sweep. See [[Blender 3D Logo Pipeline]] for the approved art and lessons.
- The first square icon needed work: an oversized glossy gold plane and cropped avatar did not match the gameplay-oriented reference icons he supplied. Do not infer that approval of the logo means every asset should have the same glossy close-up treatment.
- Icon references Holden selected: Steal An Egg, +1 Loot To Forge, +1 Stone Skipping, Build the Pyramid, and Ride A Pet. Direction: recognizably Roblox avatar and action, clear game item, readable progression cues, vivid simple environment.
- The revised icon uses a throwing avatar on a floating island, a white starter plane, cloud bounces, and +1 labels. It is a review candidate, not yet approved. See [[Paper Plane Toss Thumbnails and Game Icon]].

## Further icon critique (2026-10-03)

- Holden said the two large banners were fine; the square icon was still lacking. Keep those deliverables separate when responding to critique.
- Rejected v2's sparse composition led to a tighter v3: larger throwing avatar, plane moved up beside the character, smaller cliff, one foreground cloud bounce and one bold +1.
- The reference icons' useful qualities are compressed action, recognizable Roblox proportions, strong silhouette separation, and legible progression cues. Accurate subject matter alone is insufficient if the framing lacks energy.
- v3 is awaiting review. Details, comparison images and provenance: [[Paper Plane Toss Thumbnails and Game Icon]].

## Selected marketing art must match the game (2026-10-03)

- Holden picked the How Far banner, original v4 Noob Pro banner, and the icon with both +1 labels, then requested an environment correction across all three.
- Minecraft-style cube grass, studs, cubic terrain edges, and cube trees are wrong for this game's smooth pastel low-poly island. Use organic continuous grass surfaces, simple faceted beige undersides, rounded layered canopies, hanging vines, and the real cloud lane.
- Preserve selected characters, planes, text, and framing when the requested change concerns only the environment. Use exact user-attached picks rather than the newest local candidate.
- Real game renders show a continuous cloud road with rainbow arches; earlier prompts banning any cloud road should be superseded. Use distant lane detail without competing with foreground bounce action.
- Pastel ground should not wash out the whole promotional image. Retain clear contrast on the avatar, plane, sky, and lettering.
- All before/after images and source provenance are in [[Paper Plane Toss Thumbnails and Game Icon]]. Corrected outputs await review.

## Dragon's Hoard prop set (2026-10-03)
- Holden's approved direction for a stylised treasure set: held-size props (egg 1.6 studs, sword 4.4), everything modelled in Blender, eggs with overlapping raised scale plates, a wood-and-gold chest, and a silver sword with a gold wing guard and ruby accents. See [[Dragons-Hoard-Set]].
- Self-critique fixed before Holden saw v1:
  - Kite-shaped egg plates read as flat triangles; rounded 7-point shingles fixed it.
  - Rows of alternating colour looked like "Christmas stripes"; mixing colour per scale, as a gradient, fixed it.
  - Bare mound bands made a coin pile look like a turtle shell.
  - Pale gold rendered grey; keep gold saturated.
- After v1 Holden said he "definitely" has critiques, like the ones Claude listed (octagon coins, plain lid underside, flat small pile, thin crown points), and parked the set. Get his list before v2.
