# Roblox Development Playbook

Start with [[CLAUDE]] and [[Roblox Vault Coverage and Maintenance]]. This is a navigation and decision framework; the vault is not yet a complete, validated production manual.

## How Claude should use this vault

1. Identify the project, audience, device targets, budget, approved scope and success measures. Read its project index and source-of-truth planning files.
2. Search canonical notes for the decision. Check evidence status, verification date and unresolved gaps. Separate personal preferences, historical observations and platform requirements.
3. Choose the smallest test that could change the decision. State alternatives, assumptions and what failure would look like.
4. For code, present plan plus file list for Holden's approval. Use the project's actual data/remotes/module contracts rather than inventing an incompatible framework.
5. Validate against representative phones, multiple clients and failure conditions appropriate to the change. Record actual evidence and build identity.
6. Save findings in the existing note and link project outcomes. Update coverage gaps; do not hide uncertainty with a confident checklist.

## Work through the lifecycle

| Stage | Decision to resolve | Required evidence to aim for | Starting notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept | Why will this audience play and return? | Distinct promise, core-loop prototype, observed playtest friction | [[Paper Plane Toss References and Core Loop]], [[Fish a Monster Catch and Reel Design]] |
| Design | Is progress satisfying and sustainable? | Explicit economy assumptions, paced goals, alternatives tested with players | [[Paper Plane Toss Progression Numbers]], [[Paper Plane Toss Rebirth]] |
| Build | Can players act safely and keep their progress? | Authoritative contracts, save/rejoin/failure cases, exploitable inputs rejected | [[Roblox Persistence and Session Failure Runbook]], [[Fish a Monster Saving and Offline Income]], [[Roblox Purchase Receipt Handling]] |
| Presentation | Is the game readable and appealing on target devices? | In-game art review, touch layout, asset permissions and device profiling | [[Art Direction Feedback]], [[Blender to Roblox Asset Pipeline]], [[Roblox Mobile UI Layout]], [[Roblox Audio Pipeline]] |
| Monetise | Is the offer understandable, deliverable and sustainable? | Current policy, correct prices, durable grants, buyer experience tests | [[Paper Plane Toss Monetization]], [[Roblox Purchase Receipt Handling]] |
| Launch | Can we detect and recover from failure? | Release identity, observability, rollback, support and moderation ownership | [[Paper Plane Toss Release Prep]], [[Fish a Monster Pre-Publish Review]] |
| Grow | Which bottleneck is evidenced? | Mature cohorts, acquisition-source separation, controlled experiments | [[Roblox Discovery and Retention Measurement]], [[Paper Plane Toss Thumbnails and Game Icon]] |

These are recommended evidence gates, not claims that existing projects already meet them. For missing manuals use the coverage backlog. Commercial outcomes remain uncertain; validate the proposition with players rather than treating competitor mechanics as proof.
