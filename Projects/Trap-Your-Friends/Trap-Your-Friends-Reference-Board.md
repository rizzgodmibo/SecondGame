# Trap Your Friends: Retro Style Reference Board

## TL;DR
- Pictures for Holden's style approval ([[Trap-Your-Friends-Art-Style-Guide]]). Three groups: **(1) modern hits with a retro look** (Holden's JJS lead), **(2) classic brickbattle and obby games**, **(3) devforum builds showing classic building rules and materials**.
- Contact sheets are saved locally in `Assets/Reference-Captures/Retro-Stud/` (third-party art, local only, gitignored). Remote images below load from devforum or Roblox's CDN. **Roblox thumbnail links expire after ~180 days** (`180DAY` in the URL).
- Inspection: **stills only** (thumbnails and screenshots), no gameplay watched. Thumbnails are marketing art, not the game.
- Capture method: the Browser pane screenshotted contact pages. Direct image download was blocked by the network policy. Player counts were read from the Roblox games API on 2026-10-05 and are a single snapshot.

## 1. Modern hits with a retro look (closest to what Holden means)

![[sheet-b-jjs-forsaken-utg-doomspire.jpg]]

| Game | Snapshot 2026-10-05 | What to take (obs → principle) |
|---|---|---|
| [Jujutsu Shenanigans](https://www.roblox.com/games/9391468976) (Tze's Shenanigans, 2022) | ~142k playing, 7.5B visits, Battlegrounds | Obs: in-game shots show modern lit city/station maps and normal avatars; one thumbnail shows **a wall exploding into a shower of coloured cubes** (at contact-sheet resolution the cubes look plain/smooth; **studded debris is our own design choice, not confirmed from JJS**, corrected 2026-10-05). Principle: the "retro" is in the **destructible brick** feel and blocky bodies, not 2008 lighting |
| [Forsaken](https://www.roblox.com/games/18687417158) (2024) | ~52k playing, 5.8B visits, 1-vs-All | Obs: classic blocky characters with classic faces, dark dramatic lighting, hand-drawn comic thumbnails. Principle: blocky classic characters + modern mood works |
| [Die of Death](https://www.roblox.com/games/71895508397153) (2025) | ~2.6k playing, 339M visits | Obs: comic thumbnails crowded with blocky characters and props; a card-pick UI ("Deck of Death"). Principle: goofy blocky cast + bold UI |
| [untitled tag game](https://www.roblox.com/games/14044547200) (2023) | ~1.3k playing, 440M visits | Obs: comic thumbnails of blocky noobs with exaggerated faces. Principle: classic noob silhouettes are instantly readable and funny |

## Destruction references (for the JJS-style system)
| Resource | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [VoxelDestruct 2.1](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/voxeldestruct-21-voxelated-destruction-physics-with-greedy-meshing-hitboxes-and-more/3044264) | Community module: greedy-meshed voxel destruction, `OnClient` option, `.Repair()`, reset timer (default 60 s), `RecordDestruction` for late joiners. Users compare it to JJS. ⚠️ audit code and licence before use |
| ["How can I make this effect?"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-make-this-effect/3675998) | The simple version: unanchor fragments, `ApplyImpulse`, tween size/transparency to 0, Debris cleanup |
| [Non-destructive voxel engine](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-non-destructive-voxel-destruction-engine/3980658) | Warns about main-thread hitches from parenting/welding and physics cost with thousands of parts |
| [Voxel Destruction System](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/voxel-destruction-system/3864250) | Part pooling, chunked queries, BulkMoveTo, spreading work over frames |
- **Why our case is easier:** lanes are already a 1-stud grid of bricks, so there's no voxelising step. Destroying = hiding cells on the server + spawning pooled cosmetic cubes on clients.
