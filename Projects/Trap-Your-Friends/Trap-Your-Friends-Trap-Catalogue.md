# Trap Your Friends: Trap Catalogue (brainstorm, DRAFT)

## TL;DR
- (USER, 2026-10-06) Holden: "we need **TONS** of [traps], that's the whole game", and the v1 traps "look kind of uninteresting".
- This note is a **pool of ~100 trap ideas** across 12 families, plus 4 systems that multiply variety (triggers and wiring, rarities, upgrades, combos). It's a brainstorm: nothing here is approved, costs are guesses, and nothing has been tested.
- Suggested launch target: **30–40 traps**, then **2–4 new traps every weekly update** (cheap content: one trap = one module + one model). See [[Content-Cadence]].
- Every trap must follow the **trap design rules** below. They're the fix for "uninteresting".

## Trap design rules (proposal, applies to every trap)
1. **A trap is a character, not a block.** Chunky silhouette, moving parts, and many have **googly eyes or a face** (goofy personality, fits the Forsaken/UTG comic vibe).
2. **Black cartoon outline** on every trap model (see [[Trap-Your-Friends-Style-Test-v1-Feedback]]).
3. **Idle animation** (bob, wiggle, tick, breathe) so traps feel alive while waiting.
4. **Tell → wind-up → hit → recover.** Every dangerous trap telegraphs (sound + glow + shake ~0.4 s) so deaths feel fair and funny, not random.
5. **Big readable hit:** squash-and-stretch, impact flash, sound, debris, the runner flung (not just reset).
6. **One colour family per trap family** (danger = red/orange, movement = blue/cyan, bounce = pink, sticky/slow = green, trick = purple, explosive = red-white stripes, gadget = yellow).
7. **Footprint on the grid:** 1×1, 2×2, 2×4, 4×4 or "lane-wide". Shown on the card.
8. **Counterplay exists** (timing, path choice, Bash, jump). No unavoidable instant kills.

## Systems that multiply the trap count (proposal)
1. **Wiring (triggers):** pressure plate, tripwire, timer or button → any trap with a "triggered" mode (TNT, Crusher, Trapdoor, Boxing Glove, Boulder). 5 triggers × 20 triggerable traps = lots of combos with little extra code. Big depth for build-strategy players. (v1.5 rather than launch?)
2. **Rarities and cards:** Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary trap cards. Draft from a hand each round; rare traps show up less often. Unlock by playing (XP, trap mastery), **never by paying**. Odds must be shown if any random unlock exists ([[Reward-Schedules]]).
3. **Trap upgrades in-round:** spend leftover budget to upgrade a placed trap (faster spinner, bigger blast). Simple ×1.25 stats.
4. **Themed trap packs per lane theme:** Candy Land (gumdrop bounce, chocolate river), Castle (portcullis, boiling oil), Factory (press, conveyor maze), Space (airlock, alien grabber). The same mechanic gets a new skin = cheap variety.
