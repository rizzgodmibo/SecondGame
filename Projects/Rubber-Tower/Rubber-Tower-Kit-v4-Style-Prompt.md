# Rubber Tower: kit v3 critique → v4 prompt (Roblox style + more fantasy)

Holden's critique of Valley Kit v3 (Blender screenshots, 2026-10-04). Paste the prompt below into Claude Code.

```
Kit v3 feedback: better, and you can keep these models, but I want MORE models, way more fantasy/magic, and a Roblox style. Right now it looks too realistic and muted. Read [[Art Direction Feedback]], [[Art-Direction]], [[X-Low-Poly-Builds-And-Maps]] (haooffiso's Sky_Island fantasy hub and Orca_Environ's pastel lagoon are the vibe), [[Rubber-Tower-Valley-Kit]] and the "Selected marketing art must match the game" section about the smooth pastel low-poly look before you start.

WHAT'S WRONG WITH v3
1. Too realistic and dull. The colours are beige, grey, olive and dark slate, like a real canyon. It doesn't look like a Roblox game.
2. The cliffs look like stacked boxes. They're rectangular blocks of similar size, and the dark back slabs show from the side and back.
3. The big grey rock has no colour zones at all. It's one flat grey, which I've rejected before.
4. The ground plane's lighter/darker blobs look like camo. I want crisp curved zones, not that.
5. Barely any fantasy. Apart from the beacon crystal and the summit crystal, nothing says magic.
6. Workbench renders make everything look darker and sadder than the game will.

WHAT "ROBLOX STYLE" MEANS HERE
- Chunky, soft, toy-like shapes: big simple forms, rounded/bevelled edges, exaggerated proportions (fat mushroom caps, thick tree trunks, oversized crystals and flowers). Few big shapes > many small details.
- Bright and saturated: candy colours with clean, crisp zones (grass tops, rock sides, highlight faces). Rocks can be lavender, teal or warm pink-grey, not realistic grey/beige. Keep 2–3 tones per material so it doesn't look flat.
- Still flat-shaded low-poly with crisp colour zones (my standing rule). NOT Minecraft cubes, NOT parts, NOT realistic.
- Readable from far away: strong silhouettes, glowing accents for anything magic.

NEW MODELS I WANT (keep v3, add these; 2–3 sizes/colour variants each where it makes sense)
- Magic everywhere: glowing crystal clusters (several colours), floating rune stones and obelisks with glowing runes, floating rocks with chains and crystals underneath, magic portal/rune ring, glowing spell circle for the ground, fairy lanterns on posts, floating lantern lights, a cauldron, floating spell books.
- Fantasy plants: 3+ tree species (rounded layered candy canopies, a pink blossom tree, a glowing-fruit tree, a twisted magic tree), giant mushrooms (glowing and normal), big stylised flowers, hanging vines, glowing grass tufts.
- Fantasy buildings/landmarks: a wizard tower, a small mushroom house, ruins/arches with glowing runes, a rainbow or crystal bridge, a castle for the summit area.
- Per segment, themed platforms that look like objects:
  - S1 meadow: grassy islets, flower pads, log/stump platforms.
  - S2 mushroom grove: mushroom-cap platforms, shelf-fungus wall ledges, glowing spore mushrooms.
  - S3 crystal-ice: crystal shard platforms, ice slabs, frozen arches.
  - S4 cloud kingdom: puffy cloud platforms, gold/white sky temple pieces, rainbow pieces.
- Restyle the v3 cliffs: rounder chunky rock masses in brighter fantasy colours, with grass caps and crystals growing out of them, no visible dark back slabs.

PROCESS
- Start with a style test: restyle 3 existing models (one cliff, one islet, the checkpoint beacon) plus 3 new magic models. Show me v3 vs v4 side by side and wait for my OK before making the rest.
- Write a one-page style guide in the vault first (palette hex codes per segment, accent colours for magic glow, bevel size, proportions, tri budgets) and check every model against it.
- Render with bright, game-like lighting (Eevee with a sun and blue sky), not Workbench. Also do one diorama render of everything together.
- Self-critique honestly before you show me and fix weak points first.
- Keep the pipeline rules: made in Blender by script, preflight PASS, 256² atlas, sensible tris, glow parts as separate Neon pieces/attachments in Studio.
- Don't upload until I approve the renders. Log this critique in [[Rubber-Tower]] and [[Rubber-Tower-Valley-Kit]].
```
