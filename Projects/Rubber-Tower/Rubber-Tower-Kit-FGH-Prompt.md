# Rubber Tower: batches (f) NPC shops, (g) crazy hats, (h) misc (Holden's brief)

Pasted by Holden in chat on 2026-10-04, after reviewing batches (b)–(e2). His opening line was: "redo the dragon, finish the rest of the models you have to do then."

**Batch naming:** Holden counts (a)–(e) as done. My old landmarks + backdrop batch, which I had built as "(f)", is now **(e2)**, so the letters (f), (g) and (h) are free for this brief.

**Summary of the brief:**
- **(f) NPC shops and structures:**
  - **Three shops, each with an NPC:**
    1. A cosmetics tailor tent in the hub (spends Squishies).
    2. A gamepass shop in the hub that looks premium (gold + Robux-green), with the passes shown as props: banana bunch, piggyback saddle-backpack, SKIP balloon or cannon.
    3. A halfway cosmetics stall at CP2, themed to that segment.
  - **Non-shop structures:**
    - a world portal plaza with locked/unlocked pedestals;
    - a leaderboard plaza (podium, noticeboard frame, statue spot);
    - a quest/notice board, a daily mailbox or gift chest pedestal, a codes wishing well;
    - a town crier podium with a bell (server events);
    - a tutorial hut and a "Bonk Clinic" tent;
    - a friend campfire;
    - a waystone at each checkpoint;
    - summit pieces: victory podium, confetti cannons, photo frame, "you made it" arch.
  - **Modular stall kit:** frames, counters, awnings in 6 colours, tent roofs, kiosks, blank signs for SurfaceGui, shelves, jars, crates, pedestals, price tags, Squishies piles, cash register, bell.
  - **Rules:**
    - Scaled to R15 (5–6 studs), with waist-height counters and high roofs.
    - Open fronts, so the NPC can be seen on a phone.
    - The manifest marks the NPC spot and the ProximityPrompt anchor.
    - Each structure has a clear silhouette and one strong colour.
    - NPC characters come later.
  - **Hub diorama** from player camera height, with dummies at the NPC spots.
- **(g) Crazy hats:**
  - About 30–40 hats across the rarity colours (Common grey, Uncommon green, Rare blue, Epic purple, Legendary gold, Mythic red/pink).
  - Higher tiers get more detail, glow and wobble bits.
  - The wobble parts are separate pieces, with pivots in the manifest.
  - Each hat is an Accessory (Handle + HatAttachment) of 500–2k tris on shared atlases, fit-tested on R6 and R15 with different heads and hair, and it must stay on during ragdoll.
  - One icon camera rig, plus a render on a dummy head.
  - A hat display set: stands, mannequin heads, a rack, glass cases.
  - Some hats are sold only in the halfway shop.
  - **Squishies prices: Holden decides later.**
- **(h) Extra and misc:**
  - Hub village kit, entry gates per segment, height markers, AFK seats.
  - Fantasy extras and secrets.
  - Gameplay items: placeable banana peel, Squishies pickup, summit crown, trophies, title plaque, checkpoint flags.
  - Goofy stuff, ground clutter, village life, magic clutter, per-segment extras, a seasonal swap set.
- **Process:**
  1. The checklist goes in [[Rubber-Tower-Valley-Kit]] first, skipping anything that exists (reuse it or recolour it).
  2. Same v5 rules; references next to every model; variants over unique meshes; preflight PASS.
  3. **Stop for review after (f), after (g) and after (h).** A diorama per batch.
  4. **Don't upload or build the map yet.**
