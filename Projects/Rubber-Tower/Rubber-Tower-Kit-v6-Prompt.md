# Rubber Tower: v5 approved style → v6 prompt (full kit)

Holden's verdict on the v5 previews (2026-10-04): the style works; now he wants far more models, obby-mechanic models and more fantasy. Paste everything in the block into Claude Code.

```
v5 feedback: we can work with this style. Lock it in. The problem now is quantity: I want WAY more models. We definitely need the obby pieces (ice, bouncy, wobble platforms and so on) and a lot more fantasy models in general.

STEP 0: LOCK THE STYLE
- Update the style guide in the vault so v5 is the approved look: chunky faceted rock with dark cracks and edge highlights, bright grass caps with tufts hanging over the edges, crisp painted zones, little pebbles/flowers/props for detail, a goofy slightly crooked feel. Every new model must match the v5 wall, islet, beacon and crystal.
- Small fixes on the v5 pieces:
  - wall: the rock masses are too even, like a grid; vary their sizes and push some out further;
  - beacon: the blue brick pattern is too busy and flat; use chunkier stones with fewer, bigger bricks;
  - islet bush: make it a puffy canopy.
- Keep pulling from the vault references (OmniKoi2, haooffiso Sky_Island, Mossy Rocks, Orca_Environ, and anything in Assets/Reference-Captures/Holden-Picks/). Put the reference next to each new model in the render sheets.

STEP 1: OBBY MECHANIC MODELS (most important)
Every mechanic gets its own instantly readable look and colour, so players know what a platform does before they touch it ([[Obby-Special-Platforms]], ideas in [[Rubber-Tower-Ideas-Bank]]). 2–3 sizes each, a flat, fair standing top, and fit the existing tags and code (IcePlatform, WobblePlatform, BouncePad; wobble platforms ~12×12 with room underneath for the springs).
- Bounce: mushroom trampolines, flower pads, jelly/slime pads, a springy coil pad, a giant bouncy leaf.
- Ice: ice slabs, frozen puddle islets, icicle-edged crystal ledges, a slippery ice slide/ramp.
- Wobble: jelly cubes, wobbly lily pads, a seesaw log, a floating raft on chains, a spring-mounted stone disc.
- Soft rest platforms: puffy cloud pads, giant pillows/marshmallows, a hay bale, a moss cushion (soft landing, never a spawn).
- Movement pieces for future mechanics: a wobbly rope bridge, a tightrope, swinging rubber paddles/hammers, a windmill, fans/wind vents, a slingshot launcher, conveyor-like vine belts.
- Climbing pieces: ladders, vine walls, stair-step mushrooms up a wall, shelf-fungus ledges, wooden scaffolding, stone steps spiralling round a pillar.
- Checkpoint pieces: a checkpoint island per segment (themed), the beacon variants, a "soft fall" net.

STEP 2: PLATFORMS PER SEGMENT (each segment looks like its own world)
- S1 meadow: grassy islets, log and stump platforms, flower pads, wooden planks, picket fences, haystacks, a windmill.
- S2 mushroom grove: mushroom-cap platforms in several colours, shelf-fungus wall ledges, glowing spore mushrooms, giant toadstools with doors and windows, roots and vines.
- S3 crystal-ice: crystal shard platforms, ice slabs, frozen arches, snowy islets, a frozen waterfall, icicles.
- S4 cloud kingdom: puffy cloud platforms, gold and white sky-temple pieces, rainbow bridges, floating pillars, a sky castle gate.

STEP 3: FANTASY MODELS (lots, with colour and size variants)
- Magic: crystal clusters in 5+ colours, floating rune stones and obelisks, floating chained rocks, a portal, spell circles, potion bottles, a cauldron, floating spell books, a wizard's hat on a post, magic orbs on pedestals, glowing rune doors.
- Buildings/landmarks: a wizard tower, mushroom houses, a fairy treehouse, ruined arches and pillars, a crooked lighthouse, a floating castle, an airship outside the walls, a giant goofy statue (e.g. a rubber duck or a wobbly knight).
- Plants: 4+ tree species (puffy canopy, pink blossom, glowing-fruit, twisted magic tree, crystal tree), giant flowers, bushes, vines, lily pads, glowing grass.
- Creatures as decor (no gameplay): small friendly slimes, a sleeping dragon on a far peak, birds/butterflies, a frog on a lily pad.
- Props: lanterns, signposts with silly signs, treasure chests, barrels, crates, banners and flags, benches, a fountain, bridges.
- Backdrop: more cliff and wall variants, waterfalls, distant floating islands, distant mountains, clouds.

PROCESS
1. Before modelling, write the full kit list into [[Rubber-Tower-Valley-Kit]] as a checklist with each model's size, tri budget, references and which segment uses it.
2. Build in batches and stop for my review after each: (a) obby mechanic models, (b) S1 + fantasy props, (c) S2, (d) S3, (e) S4 + backdrop. Show a render sheet per batch (model | reference) plus one diorama shot of the batch together, with a Roblox-avatar-sized dummy for scale.
3. Reuse meshes with colour and size variants where it makes sense ([[X-Low-Poly-Builds-And-Maps]], M0TOPRINCESS), so we get variety without hundreds of unique meshes.
4. Keep it phone-friendly: preflight PASS, sensible tris, shared atlas, glow pieces split out for Neon/lights in Studio, simple invisible colliders planned for platforms.
5. Self-critique each batch against the v5 style and the references before showing me, and fix weak pieces first.
6. Don't upload or build the map yet. Log this in [[Rubber-Tower]] and [[Rubber-Tower-Valley-Kit]].
```
