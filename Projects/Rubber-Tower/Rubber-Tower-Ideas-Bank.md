# Rubber Tower: Ideas Bank (brainstorm, nothing approved)

## TL;DR
- **Update (Holden, later 2026-10-04):** currency instead of XP; titles unlock from checkpoints, worlds, first cosmetic, events, and unique actions (invite 4+ friends, push 5+ down, reach the top without claiming checkpoints); Buddy Carry needs an accept prompt; banana peel 30 s cooldown, one at a time; shove close range, 30 s cooldown (changed from 45). Details in [[Rubber-Tower]].
- **Holden's review (2026-10-04):** likes the wobble/bounce areas, hand-up, Buddy Carry, banana peel, cosmetics, skip-checkpoint pass, twist events and a unique mechanic per world. Wants soft platforms between big checkpoints, 3 free emotes and the rest paid, a friend boost, titles instead of badges, and no eggs for now. Unsure about speed boards. Decisions are recorded in [[Rubber-Tower]]; the rest of this note stays a brainstorm.
- Holden said his banana-peel and carry/throw ideas were just examples and asked for more, drawing on the vault. Everything below is a **recommendation / hypothesis**. Only items listed as Holden's decisions in [[Rubber-Tower]] are approved.
- **Conflict to resolve:** [[Difficulty-And-Mastery]] says never lose more than about 60 s of progress to one mistake in a casual game and put checkpoints every 3 to 5 obstacles late in a map. Holden chose 3 to 4 checkpoints on a very tall tower, so segments will be long. Options: keep 3 to 4 big checkpoints and add free soft safe platforms between them (UNDECIDED), or add more checkpoints. ⚠️ verify by playtest.
- [[Pay-To-Win-Boundaries]] says obbies accept paid skips, cosmetics, trails and effects, and reject paid power over other players. Passes that harm other players (banana peel, throw) carry backlash risk, so the safer versions are opt-in, mutual-consent, or affect only the buyer.
- Co-play is a ranking signal ([[Friend-And-Group-Play]]), so friend-based mechanics are worth more than pure trolling.

## A. Wobble and bounce areas (DRAFT)
Introduce each in a safe spot first, then test, twist and combine ([[Difficulty-And-Mastery]] sawtooth).
- **Jelly floors:** squishy platforms that dip and spring you slightly; pair with squish sounds (cf. Squishy Troll Tower in [[Rubber-Tower-Reference-Obbies]]).
- **Bounce pads:** colour-coded trampolines that launch you; a ragdoll flight and a soft landing is the joke.
- **Wobbly bridges and tightropes:** sway; you only ragdoll if you stumble.
- **Rubber paddles or windmills:** bonk you into a floppy tumble; telegraphed by colour and sound.
- **Slingshot or rubber-band launcher:** stretch and fling to the next ledge.
- **Fans and wind gusts:** push your ragdoll sideways in set rhythms.
- **Relief beats:** soft landing zones after each spike so the tower never feels like a straight ramp.
- Rules: telegraph with colour and sound; keep the first 5 minutes beatable one-handed on mobile; avoid PC-only tricks in the main path.

## B. Free player interactions (DRAFT)
- **Basic shove** (Holden's decision): short range, cooldown, capped strength; no effect on players at spawn or on a checkpoint pad; brief shove immunity after respawn (guess ⚠️ playtest).
- **Hand up:** a friendly pull to help a nearby player onto a ledge. Free, wholesome, and gives co-op a purpose.
- **Emotes and cheers:** wave, cheer, faint; cheap social glue.
- **Friends on the height bar:** HUD shows friends' height on the tower (co-play signal).
- **Optional shove setting:** off, friends only, or everyone (UNDECIDED).

## C. Gamepasses and products (DRAFT, nothing priced)
Prices must be checked against current Roblox rules ⚠️ verify ([[Gamepasses-vs-Developer-Products]]).
- **Self-affecting or cosmetic (low risk):** rubber trails, squeak or boing sound packs, jiggly hats, confetti on checkpoints, funny fall poses, rainbow shove effect, emotes.
- **Buddy Carry (Holden's idea, safer version):** pick up a friend to carry or throw only if they accept; thrown players land safely and never lose a checkpoint. Reads as co-op, not grief.
- **Banana peel (Holden's idea, safer version):** one active at a time, short lifetime, not placed near checkpoints or the summit, slips you a short distance and never past your current checkpoint.
- **Skip checkpoint (developer product):** the standard obby product; prompt after repeated falls, at most once per segment. Decide whether a skip counts toward beating the tower and unlocking worlds (UNDECIDED).
- **Springy shoes or low-gravity pass:** accepted in obbies per the vault, but they change the difficulty, so decide whether they are allowed on the main run or only in a fun mode (UNDECIDED).
- **Private servers:** cheap co-play perk.
- **Avoid:** paid random items such as eggs (rejected in obbies and policy-restricted for some players), and any paid item that lets a payer knock others far down the tower.

## D. Retention and server-wide fun (DRAFT)
- **Badges:** each checkpoint, summit, first shove, first bounce, and secrets (Troll Ragdoll Tower has 90+, see [[Rubber-Tower-Reference-Obbies]]).
- **Server-wide twist events:** short modifiers such as low gravity for 60 seconds (Tower of Hell sells similar "mutators"; here they would be free and timed). Needs care that they cannot be bought to grief.
- **Daily or weekly challenge:** a rotating wobble variant on one segment (UNDECIDED).
- **World select:** new worlds unlock after beating the first (Holden's decision); each world adds a signature wobble mechanic.
- **Speed boards:** best time to each checkpoint and to the summit ([[Leaderboards]]).

## Pitfalls
- Trolling is the draw in the references, but young teens and policy reviews mean griefing must be capped.
- Too many new mechanics before launch: pick two or three for world 1.
