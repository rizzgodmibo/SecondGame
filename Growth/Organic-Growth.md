# Organic Growth (Short-form Video, Community, Update Cadence, the Home Flywheel)

## TL;DR
- **The flywheel:** outside traffic (shorts, friends, Discord, ads) puts you in RFY *retrieval*. Then RFY-sourced players' PTR, bounce, play days and playtime decide expansion. More RFY players means more co-play invites and more content creators, and the cycle repeats. Off-platform buzz **starts** it, retention **keeps it turning**. See [[Discovery-Algorithm]].
- **Short-form video is the #1 free acquisition channel** for Roblox (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels). Post **1–3 clips a day** in launch weeks. Each clip shows **one surprising moment in the first second**, is 9:16, has captions, and ends on the game name. Make the game *clip-able* by design: big reveals, rare drops, funny physics, social chaos.
- **Track every off-platform link with Share Links** (unlimited per game, per-channel analytics: D7, 7-day playtime, 30-day revenue/user). A share link that brings **new or lapsed Roblox users** can earn **Creator Rewards Audience Expansion**: 35% of their first $100 of qualifying purchases over 60 days, if the game holds ≥100 DAU on average.
- **Own the community:** a Roblox **group** (official "best way" to inform players; group-owned games) plus Discord/Guilded. Codes, update teasers and polls drive returning play days, which are the core RFY signal since June 2026.
- **Update cadence:** a **weekly-to-biweekly** content drop in the first 2–3 months, then biweekly or monthly with events. Each update triggers an explore wave. Pair every update with new thumbnails, an event listing, a notification and a Today's Picks nomination. See [[Live-Ops-Playbook]].
- **Trend-jack fast, add a twist.** Roblox trends run in weeks, not months. Ship a trend-relevant game or update within **1–3 weeks** of a meme peak, with a unique spin (an official discovery best practice).

## Details

### Channel table
| Channel | Effort | Best for | Key tactics | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok / YT Shorts / Reels** | Med (daily) | New users, virality | Hook in 0–1 s; native Roblox footage with exaggerated reactions; trending audio; text overlay "this Roblox game lets you…"; pin a comment with the game name (TikTok links are limited) | Share-link clicks, "Other" source spikes, search lift for your name |
| **YouTube long-form (yours)** | High | Devlogs, trust, older players | Devlog series with 1 video per update | Share link in the description |
| **Influencers** | Varies | Spikes, Charts entry | See [[Influencer-Coverage]] | Per-creator share links / codes |
| **Roblox group** | Low | Returning users, announcements | Group shout per update; group-only perks (chat tag, +10% coins) to drive joins | Group members, group-join prompt conversion |
| **Discord / Guilded** | Med (moderation) | Core fans, feedback, codes | #announcements, #codes, #bug-reports, #suggestions; weekly event or AMA; roles for testers | Returning DAU after posts |
| **X/Twitter** | Low | Dev community, press, trackers | Teasers, update countdowns; reply to Bloxy News/RTC-type accounts | Share link |
| **In-game referral and invites** | Low (one-time build) | Co-play signal plus new users | Friend referral system (`ReferredByPlayerId`), invite prompts, reward both sides. See [[Sharing-And-Referral-Loops]] | Co-play days in Home Recs tab |
| **Experience events and notifications** | Low | Lapsed and returning | Event listing (up to 5 thumbnails), opt-in prompts | Event RSVPs, resurrected users |
| **Curation nominations** | Low | Free Home slots | Today's Picks on Home (renominate each notable update); Standout Games (novel games) | "Today's Picks" acquisition source |

### Making the game clip-able (design inputs)
- **Moments that surprise in 3 seconds**: rare or secret items with screen-wide VFX, absurd scale (huge pets, 1e100 numbers), slapstick physics, betrayal or steal mechanics (the "Steal a Brainrot" pattern), dramatic reveals.
- **Visible social stakes**: other players around, leaderboards, trading.
- **Recording affordances**: a hide-UI toggle, a photo mode or freecam-like spectator, clean 9:16-safe framing for key moments.
- **Shareable codes and seasonal hooks** give creators a reason to post ("NEW CODES").

### Trend-jacking rules
1. Monitor: Charts → Top Trending and Up-and-Coming daily; TikTok #roblox; trackers (Bloxy News, RTC); stats sites (see [[Genre-Positioning]]).
2. Decide within **48 h**: does the trend fit your genre and can you ship a quality version in ≤2 weeks?
3. Add **one twist** (mechanic, setting or combination). Roblox explicitly de-prioritises non-unique metadata and place files.
4. Ship the update with a trend-aware title tag and thumbnail, and post 3–5 clips in 72 h.
5. Retain: convert trend tourists into play-day habits (dailies, collections) before the trend fades. Recent mega-hits peaked within a few months of release: Grow a Garden reached 21.3M CCU (Jun 2025) and 22.35M (Aug 23 2025); Steal a Brainrot reached 25.87M (Oct 11 2025, Guinness).

### Update cadence and the "front page" effect
- Official: updates are one of the 4 factors that move Home impressions. Roblox describes an **explore spike after a content update**, then expansion only if the cohort retains.
- Practical cadence (community consensus ⚠️ verify against your own analytics): weeks 1–8 weekly; months 3–6 every 1–2 weeks; mature games every 2–4 weeks plus seasonal events. Release on **Friday or Saturday US afternoon** to ride the Saturday Home peak. See [[Launch-Checklist]].
- Each update bundle: new content + new thumbnail set + title tag + event listing + notification + group shout + Discord ping + 3 clips + Today's Picks form.

### Creator Rewards (official, replaced Creator Affiliate on 2025-07-24)
| Reward | Rule |
|---|---|
| Daily Engagement | **5 Robux/day** per *Active Spender* (≥$9.99 spent in 60 d) whose day's first-3 games include yours with **≥10 min** of play |
| Audience Expansion: share link | New or Reactivated (60 d lapsed) user joins Roblox via your **Share Link**, plays ≥10 min that day → **35% of their first $100** of qualifying purchases (Robux/Premium/subscriptions) over 60 days, if your game averages **≥100 DAU** for 60 days |
| Audience Expansion: direct link / name search | The same 35% when they arrive via your direct link or a search for your exact game name |
Payouts are "Earned Robux" with a 60-day hold. Implication: **off-platform marketing that recruits *new-to-Roblox* users pays twice.**

## Checklist
- [ ] Share links created per channel (TikTok, YT, Discord, X, each influencer).
- [ ] Group created; game group-owned; social links set on the game page.
- [ ] Discord with announcements, codes, bugs, suggestions; moderation bots; 13+ rules.
- [ ] Clip backlog of 20 clips before launch; posting schedule of 1–3/day for 14 days.
- [ ] Referral and invite prompts live with rewards for both sides.
- [ ] Update bundle template (above) used every release.
- [ ] Weekly review: share-link cohort D7 and revenue vs Home Recs cohort.

## Pitfalls
- Viral clips that misrepresent the game cause a bounce spike, which hurts the RFY cohort. The clip must show the real loop.
- Codes that require off-platform actions or Robux-giveaway framing in metadata lead to reduced exposure ⚠️ verify: current Community Standards on social-follow rewards.
- Discord as the only channel. Most Roblox players are young and many don't use Discord. In-game group and notifications reach more.
- Updating for updates' sake. A broken or rushed update hurts D2–7 and D8–28 and shrinks distribution.
- Chasing a trend after its peak. The window is often 2–6 weeks ⚠️ verify per trend.
