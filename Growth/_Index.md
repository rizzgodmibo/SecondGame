# Growth: Index (Acquisition and Discovery)

## TL;DR
- Roblox growth = **get tested** (any traffic puts you in RFY retrieval) + **pass the test** (RFY-sourced players' play-through, first-play bounce, play days and playtime over 28 days) + **repeat** with each update. Read [[Discovery-Algorithm]] first.
- Fix the **first 60 seconds and D1** before you spend on ads or influencers. Ad-acquired players never count toward RFY ranking.
- The store page (icon, thumbnails, title, description) is a *promise*. Make it exciting **and true**, because mis-selling raises bounce and shrinks distribution.
- Stack launches and updates into a 24–48 h window on **Fri/Sat US time**. Home traffic peaks on Saturday.
